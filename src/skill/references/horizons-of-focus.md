# Horizons of Focus

GTD's vertical focus. The horizontal axis ensures "nothing slips"; the vertical axis ensures "you're doing the right things".

| Altitude | Name | Question it answers | List |

## Why most people get stuck
Stuck firefighting between the runway and 10k, never looking up to 30k+. The result is **efficiently doing things they shouldn't be doing**. The Horizons check at the end of the Weekly Review forces you to look up: ask each project "which 20k area of focus / 30k goal does it serve?" A project with no answer should be cut or re-evaluated.

## Cadence
- Runway / 10k: daily (engage) / weekly (review)
- 20k / 30k: checked once in each Weekly Review
- 40k / 50k: revisit quarterly or every six months (any time there's a major change)
